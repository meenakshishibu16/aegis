New AI chips cut inference costs in half

A new generation of accelerator chips announced this week promises to cut the cost of running large language models roughly in half, according to early benchmarks.

The chips use a larger on-die memory and a new low-precision number format, which reduces how often weights must be fetched from external memory.

Analysts expect cloud providers to roll out instances based on the chips early next year, with pricing yet to be announced.
